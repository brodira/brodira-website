What we embroider

An embroidered logo stays visible after many washes and looks more premium than a print. That is why embroidery is the usual choice for clothing that is worn every day.

  • Polo shirts and shirts with chest logo
  • Work and professional clothing that you supply or that we source
  • Name or function additionally on the chest or sleeve
  • Organic cotton blanks by Stanley/Stella on request
  • Neutral delivery to several locations or employees

How it works

You send us your logo, ideally as a vector file (AI, EPS, PDF or SVG). We digitise it as an embroidery file, agree size, position and thread colours with you, and send a sample for approval before the run. New employees later? The embroidery file stays saved, so on reorders everything looks like the first time.

Embroidery or print?

For textiles that are worn and washed often we recommend embroidery. BRODIRA embroiders; we do not print. More on the page Embroidery or print.
