Frequently asked questions

Where is BRODIRA based?

In Karlsruhe, Auer Str. 48 A, 76227 Karlsruhe. We embroider there in our own workshop.

Do you deliver across Germany?

Yes, insured throughout Germany, Austria and Switzerland. Pickup in Karlsruhe is possible by arrangement.

From what quantity do you embroider?

From 1 piece. Volume pricing applies to larger quantities.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 3–5 working days.

How quickly do I receive a quote?

Usually within one working day.

What does logo embroidery cost?

The price depends on stitch count, size, colours, garment and quantity. You receive a transparent quote with volume pricing.

Do you digitise my logo?

Yes. We create the embroidery file (punching) from your logo. On reorders with the same file the setup does not apply.

Which file do I need for my logo?

Ideally a vector file (AI, EPS, PDF, SVG). Good PNG or JPG files work too.

Do I get a sample in advance?

Yes, on request you receive an embroidery sample for approval before the run.

Which textiles do you embroider?

Polos, shirts, caps, bucket hats, bags and baby textiles, including from Stanley/Stella, Russell, SOL'S and Result. Hoodies, sweaters and other textiles on request.

What are your textiles made of?

By default organic cotton by Stanley/Stella, embroidered with Madeira thread. Which certificates apply to these materials is listed on our materials page.

Do you also embroider names?

Yes, names and lettering on towels, bibs, blankets, hats and other textiles, also as single pieces.

Do you deliver neutrally to my customers (white-label)?

Yes, on request neutrally to your locations, branches or customers.

Can I reorder?

Yes. We save your embroidery file, so the embroidery looks the same on every reorder.

Do you offer printing?

No. BRODIRA specialises in embroidery.

Do you issue invoices for companies?

Yes, business customers are welcome. For recurring orders we agree fixed terms.
