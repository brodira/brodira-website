Merch that lasts

Embroidered logos are wash-resistant and look more premium than a print. That makes caps, bucket hats, tote bags and polos merch that people keep wearing after the event. We embroider your textiles with your logo and deliver neutrally or in your packaging on request.

  • Caps, bucket hats, tote bags, polos and shirts with logo
  • Textiles by Stanley/Stella, Russell, SOL'S and Result; other blanks on request
  • Volume pricing for larger quantities, orders from 1 piece
  • Embroidery sample for approval before the run
  • Reorders with the same embroidery file, without a new setup

How to plan event merch

Tell us garment, quantity, logo and your desired date. We usually get back to you within one working day with a quote. Delivery usually takes 3–5 working days after approval; plan some buffer for larger quantities.

Frequently asked questions

Can you embroider merch for trade fairs and events?

Yes. We embroider caps, bucket hats, tote bags, polos and shirts with your logo, from single pieces to larger runs.

How fast is embroidered event merch ready?

Delivery usually takes 3–5 working days. You usually receive a quote within one working day.

Is there a minimum order?

We embroider from 1 piece. Volume pricing applies to larger quantities.

Can we reorder later?

Yes. We save your embroidery file; on reorders with the same logo the setup does not apply and the embroidery looks identical.

Which textiles can you embroider?

Caps, bucket hats, tote bags, polos and shirts, including from Stanley/Stella, Russell, SOL'S and Result. Other textiles on request.

Where is BRODIRA based?

In Karlsruhe, Auer Str. 48 A, 76227 Karlsruhe. We deliver to Germany, Austria and Switzerland.
