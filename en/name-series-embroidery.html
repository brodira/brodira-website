Name series

Embroidered names in series: send your list, we embroider every piece individually

You have a list of people: a team, a club, a nursery or the newborns of a month. You send us the list, we check it, you approve a check sheet and a sample, and we embroider every piece with the right name. From 1 piece, no minimum quantity.

Send enquiry

The concept

A name series is an order in which every piece carries a different name. Instead of sending each name separately by email, you fill in an Excel list that we send you with the quote: one row per piece with name, size, colour and position. We check the list before anything is embroidered, and a check sheet shows you exactly how each name will appear on the piece. This way you know in advance what you will receive, and we avoid mix-ups and spelling mistakes.

How it works

  1. List: You fill in our Excel template, which you receive with the quote (name, size, colour, position, note).
  2. Check: We flag duplicate names, special characters and names that do not fit at the desired position.
  3. Check sheet: You receive a PDF showing every name exactly as it will be embroidered, and approve it in writing.
  4. Sample: We embroider one piece and send you a photo for approval.
  5. Series: We embroider all pieces, sorted by size and colour, and compare every piece with your list.
  6. Delivery: Pickup in Karlsruhe by arrangement or shipping, also neutrally to your recipients.

Costs and quote

We do not state fixed prices here, because they depend on the garment, logo, quantity and number of names. We show the name separately per piece in the quote. Volume pricing applies to larger quantities, as with all our orders. The binding quote states garment, colour, size and logo.

  • Standard font: included. Own font or special letter shape: additional digitising, the price is stated in the quote.
  • New logo: one-off digitising, which does not apply on follow-up orders.
  • List check and check sheet: one-off per order, shown in the quote.
  • Shipping: by weight, shown in the quote.

Who it is for

  • Practices, companies and trades: teamwear with names.
  • Clubs and teams: player names on polos and shirts.
  • Nurseries and schools: textiles with the children's names.
  • Baby retail: personalised baby textiles from a customer list.
  • Municipalities, clinics and employers: birth gifts with the child's name.

Safety with name lists

  • Spelling: We embroider names exactly as you approve them on the check sheet. Changes after approval are charged.
  • Legibility: Letters are at least about 1 cm high. If a name does not fit at the desired position, we report it during the check.
  • No mix-ups: Every piece is packed with a slip showing its list row and checked against the check sheet before shipping.
  • Data protection: We use your list only for your order. Please send only the data that is needed for the embroidery.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a minimum quantity?

There is no minimum number of pieces. Volume pricing applies to larger quantities, as with all our orders. We tell you in the quote from which order value a name list makes sense.

What does a name series cost?

That depends on the garment, logo, quantity and number of names. Send us your list or an estimate of the quantity and you receive a quote, usually within one working day.

Which font do you use?

We embroider names in our standard font. For your own font or a special letter shape we digitise for an additional fee, which is stated in the quote.

How long can a name be?

Letters are at least about 1 cm high. How many characters fit at the desired position is checked during the list check, and we report names that do not fit.

Who is responsible for spelling mistakes?

We embroider names exactly as you approve them on the check sheet. Changes after approval are charged.

How long does it take?

We state the delivery time in the quote. It depends on whether we have the garments in stock or have to order them.

What happens to our name list?

We use the list only for your order. Please send only the data that is needed for the embroidery.
