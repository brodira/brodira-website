How to wash and care for embroidered clothing

Embroidered logos are wash-resistant. A few simple rules keep the embroidery looking good for a long time: wash inside out, not too hot, dry gently and iron from the reverse.

The most important care tips

Always check the care label of the garment first. These general rules help the embroidery:

  • Wash embroidered items inside out
  • Wash gently, not too hot (30 to 40 °C is usually enough)
  • No chlorine bleach, use a mild detergent
  • Do not spin too fast and do not wring hard
  • Air dry or use a gentle dryer programme
  • Iron from the reverse or place a cloth over the embroidery

Why embroidery lasts

The thread is anchored firmly in the fabric, unlike a print. We use Madeira premium thread, which is suitable for frequent washing. On work and team clothing the embroidery therefore lasts through many washes.

Frequently asked questions

Can embroidered clothing be washed?

Yes. Embroidered logos are wash-resistant. It is best to wash embroidered items inside out and not too hot.

Can I put embroidered clothing in the dryer?

Usually on a gentle programme; follow the care label of the garment. Air drying is the gentlest option.

Can you iron over embroidery?

Better to iron from the reverse or place a cloth over the embroidery so it is not flattened.

Which thread do you use?

Madeira premium embroidery thread.

Does embroidery last longer than printing?

Embroidered logos are wash-resistant and very durable, which is why they are popular for work and team clothing.
