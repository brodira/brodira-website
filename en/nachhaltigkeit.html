The materials we use

The manufacturer of our Stanley/Stella blanks is GOTS-certified (Stanley and Stella SA, licence CB-GOTS-CUC-03-819434, Control Union Certifications). Madeira holds an OEKO-TEX® STANDARD 100 certificate for its embroidery threads (no. 94.0.6007, Hohenstein).

  • Blanks: organic cotton by Stanley/Stella, manufacturer's GOTS certificate
  • Threads: Madeira, manufacturer's OEKO-TEX® STANDARD 100 certificate
  • Production in Karlsruhe
  • Neutral packaging on request

The certificates belong to the manufacturers and cover their materials. BRODIRA is not GOTS- or OEKO-TEX®-certified, and our embroidered products do not carry a GOTS or OEKO-TEX® label.

Why organic cotton?

Organic cotton comes from organic farming. For the Stanley/Stella blanks, this is covered by the manufacturer's GOTS certification.

Materials – FAQ

What does GOTS mean?

The Global Organic Textile Standard is the leading label for textiles made from organically grown natural fibres – with ecological and social criteria across the entire supply chain.

Which embroidery threads do you use?

Embroidery threads by Madeira. The manufacturer holds an OEKO-TEX® STANDARD 100 certificate for them (no. 94.0.6007).
