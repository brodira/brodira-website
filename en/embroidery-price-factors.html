What does embroidery cost at BRODIRA? Guide values

As a guide: a garment with an embroidered logo usually costs between €10 and €75 per piece with us, depending on the garment and quantity, including embroidery in one position, plus shipping.

  • Bags and beanies: usually approx. €10–15
  • Polo shirts, T-shirts and shirts: usually approx. €25–40
  • Jackets: usually approx. €45–75
  • Embroidering a garment you bring (single piece): usually approx. €15–30

A one-off digitising fee for your logo comes on top and does not apply on reorders with the same file. Larger quantities get volume pricing.

Guide values, as of October 2026, not binding prices. Stitch count, colours, garment and quantity set the price, so we prepare a personal quote for every enquiry, usually within one working day. A minimum order value may apply to very small orders. We are a small business under § 19 UStG and do not charge VAT.

What the price of logo embroidery depends on

The price depends on the effort for your motif and on the quantity. These factors decide:

  • Stitch count, size and level of detail of the logo
  • Number of thread colours (up to 15 possible)
  • Garment and brand: polo, shirt, cap, bag, hoodie or baby textile
  • Quantity: volume pricing applies to larger quantities
  • Embroidery position and number of embroidery spots
  • One-off digitising (setup) of the logo; it does not apply on reorders with the same file

How to get an exact quote quickly

Send us your logo, the desired garment, the quantity, the embroidery position and your desired date. You usually receive a transparent quote with volume pricing within one working day, on request with an embroidery sample for approval before the run.

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost to have a logo embroidered?

The price depends on stitch count, size, colours, garment and quantity. We prepare a transparent quote for you, usually within one working day.

Is there volume pricing?

Yes. For larger quantities the price per piece drops. We state the tiers in the quote.

Is there a setup fee?

A setup fee may apply for the one-off digitising of the logo and is shown separately in the quote. It does not apply on follow-up orders with the same embroidery file.

Are these binding prices?

No, they are guide values. Stitch count, colours, garment and quantity set the final price. We state the exact price in your personal quote.

How quickly do I receive a quote?

Usually within one working day.

Can I order from 1 piece?

Yes. We embroider from 1 piece.
