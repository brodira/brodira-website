Your situation

Maternity hospitals, birth centres and midwives want to give young families something personal. The child's name is often only known after the birth, and demand varies from week to week.

What BRODIRA takes over

We embroider blankets, hats and towels, with names or with your logo.

  • Welcome sets of blanket, hat and card, individually wrapped on request
  • Clinic or practice logo, placed discreetly
  • Name embroidery as soon as the name is final
  • Consistent quality from delivery to delivery

When the name is not yet final

We only embroider a name once it is final. For day-to-day operation, sets with your logo that you keep in stock work well. Sets with names are ordered after the birth; production usually takes 3–5 working days after approval.

Quantity, lead time and process

You can order from 1 piece; volume pricing applies to larger quantities. For regular demand we agree fixed terms and delivery dates with you.

  1. Request: You tell us the garment, quantity and desired motif. The quote usually arrives within one working day.
  2. Embroidery file and sample: We digitise your logo or motif as an embroidery file and send a sample for approval before the run.
  3. Production: Once you approve, we usually embroider within 3–5 working days.
  4. Shipping: Insured to Germany, Austria and Switzerland, in neutral packaging on request. Pickup in Karlsruhe by arrangement.
  5. Reorders: We save the embroidery file, so every reorder looks like the first one.

Example flow (for illustration)

A clinic wants to give every family a welcome set. We create the embroidery file with the clinic logo and send a sample. After approval we produce the first run, and later the clinic reorders with the same embroidery file. This is an example flow, not a customer order.

Materials and care

We use organic cotton by Stanley/Stella and embroidery threads by Madeira. Care instructions for parents are in our care tips.
