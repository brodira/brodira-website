Minimum order for embroidery: is a single piece possible?

Many embroiderers require minimum quantities. At BRODIRA you can order from 1 piece. Volume pricing applies to larger quantities, and the setup is not charged again on reorders.

From 1 piece · 3–5 working days · Sample before the run · Quote usually within 1 working day

Why are there minimum quantities at all?

An embroidery file has to be created for every logo and the machine has to be set up. For very small quantities this effort is high in relation, which is why many businesses set minimum orders.

How we work at BRODIRA

We embroider from 1 piece, whether as a personal gift, a sample or a first team outfit. Volume pricing applies to larger quantities. The one-off setup for your logo is shown separately in the quote. On reorders with the same embroidery file it does not apply.

  • Orders from 1 piece
  • Volume pricing for larger quantities
  • Saved embroidery file, identical result on reorders
  • Sample for approval before the run

Frequently asked questions

Is there a minimum order at BRODIRA?

No, you can order from 1 piece. Volume pricing applies to larger quantities.

Can I have just a single polo shirt embroidered?

Yes. Single pieces such as a polo, a towel or a bib are possible too.

Why do other embroiderers require minimum orders?

Because digitising and machine setup mean effort for every logo. We still offer single pieces and show the setup separately in the quote.

What happens on a reorder?

We save your embroidery file. On reorders with the same logo the setup does not apply.

How fast is delivery?

Usually 3–5 working days; a quote usually within one working day.
