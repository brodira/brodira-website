Frequently asked questions

Can you embroider hoodies and sweaters for start-ups?

Yes, on request. Besides polos, shirts, caps and bags we also embroider hoodies and sweaters with your logo. Tell us garment, quantity and logo; you receive a quote, usually within one working day.

Is embroidery worthwhile for a small start-up with few employees?

Yes. We embroider from 1 piece, and volume pricing applies to larger quantities. You can start with the team and reorder later without the look changing.

How quickly do we get embroidered teamwear?

Delivery usually takes 3–5 working days. You usually receive a quote within one working day, on request with an embroidery sample for approval before the run.

What do you need from us for logo embroidery?

Ideally your logo as a vector file (AI, EPS, PDF, SVG). Good PNG or JPG files work too. We digitise the logo as an embroidery file and agree size, position and thread colours with you.

What happens when we equip new employees later?

We save your embroidery file. On reorders with the same logo the setup does not apply and the embroidery looks identical.

Which textiles can you embroider for start-ups?

Polos, shirts, caps, bucket hats and bags, including from Stanley/Stella, Russell, SOL'S and Result. Other textiles such as sweaters on request.

What is the teamwear made of?

By default organic cotton by Stanley/Stella, embroidered with Madeira thread. Other brands or your own goods are possible on request.

Where is BRODIRA based?

We embroider in Karlsruhe (Auer Str. 48 A, 76227 Karlsruhe) and also deliver throughout Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

Names on every piece?

With our name list we embroider every piece individually: no minimum quantity, with check sheet and sample. See name series
