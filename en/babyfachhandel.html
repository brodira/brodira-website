Your situation

You run a baby specialty shop or online store and look for gifts that are not in every shop. Personalised textiles with names are exactly that, without you having to set up your own embroidery.

What BRODIRA takes over

We embroider in Karlsruhe and deliver ready to go, directly to your customers if you like.

  • Name and motif embroidery on blankets, hooded towels, hats, bibs and birthday crowns
  • Embroidery file in your font and colour, matching your brand
  • Neutral delivery (white-label) and packaging by arrangement
  • Permanent line or time-limited campaign, for example at Easter or Christmas

Quantity, lead time and process

You can order from 1 piece; volume pricing applies to larger quantities. This is how an order works:

  1. Request: You tell us the garment, quantity and desired motif. The quote usually arrives within one working day.
  2. Embroidery file and sample: We digitise your logo or motif as an embroidery file and send a sample for approval before the run.
  3. Production: Once you approve, we usually embroider within 3–5 working days.
  4. Shipping: Insured to Germany, Austria and Switzerland, in neutral packaging on request. Pickup in Karlsruhe by arrangement.
  5. Reorders: We save the embroidery file, so every reorder looks like the first one.

Example flow (for illustration)

A specialty shop wants to offer a line of embroidered baby blankets with names for Christmas. It sends us the font and desired colours, we create the embroidery file and a sample. Once approved we embroider the run, and every customer order in the shop can be reordered with the same motif. This is an example flow, not a customer order.

Materials and care

As textiles we use organic cotton by Stanley/Stella by default, and on request blanks by Larkwood, Babybugz and Jollein. We embroider with Madeira threads. Which certificates apply to these materials is listed on our materials page. How to wash embroidered textiles correctly is in our care tips; you can pass them on to your customers.
