Guide

Which textiles are suitable for embroidery?

Almost any firm textile can be embroidered. Cotton and blended fabrics with a stable structure work best: polos, shirts, caps, bags, towels and baby textiles.

Well suited

Stable, not too thin fabrics carry stitches cleanly. These are the textiles we embroider most often:

  • Polos and shirts: chest logo, name or lettering
  • Caps and bucket hats: logo on the front or side
  • Bags and tote bags: larger motifs
  • Towels, hooded towels and bathrobes: names and lettering
  • Baby textiles such as bibs, blankets and hats: names on organic cotton
  • Hoodies and sweaters on request

What to watch out for

Very thin, very stretchy or smooth fabrics are harder to embroider. We are happy to advise which textile suits your purpose and logo. As blanks we work with organic cotton textiles by Stanley/Stella as well as brands such as Russell, SOL'S and Result, more brands on request.

Frequently asked questions

Which clothing can be embroidered?

Polos, shirts, caps, bucket hats, bags, towels, bathrobes and baby textiles. Hoodies and sweaters on request.

Can I bring my own textiles?

Talk to us; we are happy to clarify it in the quote.

Which fabrics are unsuitable for embroidery?

Very thin, smooth or very stretchy fabrics are harder. We advise you on the right textile choice.

Do you embroider organic cotton?

Yes. By default we embroider organic cotton blanks by Stanley/Stella.
