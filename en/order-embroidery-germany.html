How an order from a distance works

We clarify everything by email or phone. You send us your logo, we take care of the rest.

  • Request: logo, garment, quantity and desired date via form, email or phone
  • Quote: usually within one working day, with volume pricing for larger quantities
  • Digitising and sample: we create the embroidery file and you approve the sample before the run
  • Production: embroidery in our workshop in Karlsruhe
  • Shipping: insured, delivery usually 3–5 working days

For companies with several locations

On request we deliver neutrally (white-label) directly to your locations, branches or customers. Business customers receive an invoice, and for recurring orders we agree fixed terms and predictable dates.

  • Delivery to several addresses possible
  • Reorders with the same embroidery file, without a new setup
  • Personal contacts instead of an anonymous large operation

Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver across Germany?

Yes. We embroider in Karlsruhe and deliver insured throughout Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

Do I have to come to Karlsruhe to order?

No. The order is handled by email or phone, and your goods are then shipped to you. Pickup in Karlsruhe is possible by arrangement.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 3–5 working days. You usually receive a quote within one working day.

How do I send you my logo?

By email to info@brodira.de, ideally as a vector file (AI, EPS, PDF or SVG). Good PNG or JPG files work too.

Can you deliver to several addresses or directly to my customers?

Yes. On request we deliver neutrally to your locations, branches or directly to your customers.

From what quantity can we order?

From 1 piece. Volume pricing applies to larger quantities.
