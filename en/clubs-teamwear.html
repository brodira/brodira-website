What suits clubs and teams

Embroidered crests and names are wash-resistant and look premium. That makes embroidery the first choice for clothing away from the pitch.

  • Club polos for board, coaches and supervisors
  • Caps and bucket hats with club crest
  • Bags and sacks for team and fans
  • Towels and bath towels with names
  • Hoodies, jackets and other textiles on request
  • Name or function under the crest, also individually

To be honest: what we do not do

BRODIRA embroiders; we do not print. Player jerseys are usually printed or flocked and therefore are not part of our offer. For club clothing that is worn and washed often, embroidery is the durable choice.

How it works

You send us the club crest, ideally as a vector file (AI, EPS, PDF or SVG). We digitise it as an embroidery file and agree size, position and thread colours. Very fine details are simplified for embroidery. Before the run you receive a sample for approval. New members? We save the embroidery file, and on reorders everything looks like the first time.

Frequently asked questions

Do you embroider jerseys for sports clubs?

No. BRODIRA specialises in embroidery and does not offer printing. For polos, caps, jackets and bags with a club crest we are the right address.

From what quantity can we order?

From 1 piece. Volume pricing applies to larger quantities.

Can individual names be embroidered under the club crest?

Yes. We embroider names and functions on individual pieces, for example for coaches or the board.

Can we reorder later for new members?

Yes. We save your embroidery file, and on reorders with the same crest the setup does not apply.

How fast is delivery?

Usually 3–5 working days after approval. You usually receive a quote within one working day.

Do you also deliver outside Karlsruhe?

Yes, insured throughout Germany, Austria and Switzerland. Pickup in Karlsruhe by arrangement.

Names on every piece?

With our name list we embroider every piece individually: no minimum quantity, with check sheet and sample. See name series
