Prepare your logo for embroidery

With the right file the embroidery goes faster and the result is better. Here you can see which files, sizes and colours work and what we need from you.

From 1 piece · 3–5 working days · Sample before the run · Quote usually within 1 working day

Request a quote

Which file is best?

Ideally a vector file (AI, EPS, PDF or SVG), which scales without loss of quality. Good high-resolution PNG or JPG files are possible too; we digitise your logo from them as an embroidery file.

  • Vector file (AI, EPS, PDF, SVG): ideal
  • PNG or JPG in high resolution: possible
  • Screenshots and heavily compressed images: please avoid
  • Lettering in the logo preferably converted to outlines or with the font name

Size, colours and position

Chest logos are usually 7–10 cm wide, slightly smaller on caps. We recommend the right size for each garment and level of detail. Up to 15 thread colours are possible, and fewer colours often look clearer. Very fine lines and colour gradients are simplified for embroidery; before the run you receive a sample for approval.

  • Left chest: the classic for polos and shirts
  • Caps: smaller logo, often front or side
  • Bags and towels: larger areas possible

What we need from you

Logo file, garment, quantity, embroidery position and desired date. We take care of the rest: digitising, sample, embroidery and shipping.

Frequently asked questions

Which file format is best for embroidery?

A vector file such as AI, EPS, PDF or SVG. Good PNG or JPG files work too.

How large can an embroidered logo be?

Chest logos are usually 7–10 cm wide, slightly smaller on caps. We recommend the right size for each garment.

How many colours are possible?

Up to 15 thread colours, with Madeira premium thread.

Do I have to create the embroidery file myself?

No. We digitise your logo as an embroidery file and agree size, position and thread colours with you.

Can I see a sample in advance?

Yes. On request you receive an embroidery sample for approval before the run.
