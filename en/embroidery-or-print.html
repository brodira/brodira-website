Embroidery or print: what suits your logo?

Embroidered or printed? For logos on polos, shirts, caps and workwear, embroidery is usually the more premium choice. For photos and colour gradients, print is better. BRODIRA embroiders; we do not print.

From 1 piece · 3–5 working days · Sample before the run · Quote usually within 1 working day

When embroidery is the better choice

Embroidered logos are three-dimensional, wash-resistant and look premium. They suit clear logos and lettering especially well.

  • Chest logos on polos and shirts
  • Caps, bucket hats and bags
  • Professional and team clothing that is washed often
  • Hotels, restaurants, practices and clinics with a uniform appearance

When print suits better

Photos, fine colour gradients and very large areas can be reproduced better with print. With very fine details a logo is simplified for embroidery. We check this during digitising and show you a sample in advance.

Frequently asked questions

Is embroidery more durable than print?

Embroidered logos are wash-resistant and long-lasting. That makes embroidery the first choice for work and team clothing.

Can any logo be embroidered?

Most logos can be embroidered. Very fine details and colour gradients are simplified during digitising. Before the run you receive a sample for approval.

Do you also offer printing?

No. BRODIRA specialises in embroidery.

Can organic cotton be embroidered?

Yes. By default we embroider organic cotton blanks by Stanley/Stella.
